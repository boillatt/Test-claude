**Sector definition: Digital Health & Health Tech (Clinical Care + Drug Development)**

Companies developing software, AI/ML models, hardware-enabled platforms, or data-driven services that improve human health, applied across two main areas:

**1. Clinical care / patient-facing digital health**
- Telemedicine and virtual care platforms
- Remote patient monitoring (wearables, sensors, connected devices)
- Clinical decision support tools (AI diagnostics, triage, imaging analysis)
- Digital therapeutics (software-based treatments, e.g. for mental health, chronic disease management)
- Care coordination / patient engagement platforms
- EHR-adjacent tools, interoperability and health data infrastructure
- Precision medicine platforms that personalize treatment using patient data

**2. Drug development / biopharma-adjacent tech**
- AI-powered drug discovery (target identification, molecule generation, protein structure prediction, generative chemistry)
- AI/ML platforms for clinical trial design, patient recruitment, or trial optimization
- Digital biomarkers: software or sensor-derived measures (e.g. from wearables, smartphone sensors, voice, imaging) used as endpoints or monitoring tools in clinical trials or disease progression tracking
- Computational biology and multi-omics platforms (genomics, proteomics) used to accelerate R&D
- Real-world data (RWD) and real-world evidence (RWE) platforms supporting regulatory or clinical decisions
- Lab automation / AI-driven experimentation platforms for pharma R&D

**Company stage/type to include:** startups through growth-stage companies (not necessarily large pharma or big tech), venture-backed where possible, since the goal is identifying investable or partnership-relevant players rather than established incumbents.

**Keywords an agent can use for search:** "AI drug discovery," "digital biomarkers," "digital therapeutics," "clinical decision support AI," "remote patient monitoring," "computational biology startup," "AI clinical trials," "precision medicine platform," "health tech startup."
